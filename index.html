Selected Work

Research Projects

01 Jun 2025 — Present

Reliability-Aware Microscopic 3D Reconstruction System for Reflective Micro-Holes

Completed

Developed a microscopic shape-from-focus metrology framework for reliable 3D reconstruction of reflective industrial micro-hole surfaces.

  • Integrated a microscopic SFF measurement system with 10× optics, polarizers, and precision translation stages.
  • Developed CycleGAN-based reflection-aware preprocessing and a reliability-aware reconstruction method incorporating focus confidence and edge-preserving regularization.
  • Reduced mean diameter deviation by 43.7% and reconstruction MAE by 56.3% compared with baseline SFF.
02 Jun 2026 — Aug 2026

Focus-Constrained Visual Predictive Control for Robotic Micro-Hole Localization

Manuscript in Preparation

Developed a macro–micro visual control framework for autonomous micro-hole localization under limited field-of-view and focus constraints.

  • Modeled a 6-DOF robot and 1D linear-stage system with an orthogonal local task frame for decoupled lateral search and axial focusing.
  • Integrated Tenengrad autofocus, bounded spiral search, and nonlinear model predictive control operating at 10 Hz.
  • Achieved an 85% search success rate for initial lateral offsets within 1.0 mm, with final localization errors below 10 μm.
03 Jan 2026 — Present

Robotic Dynamic Obstacle Avoidance Control based on Partial RGB-D Observations

Ongoing

Investigating closed-loop robotic obstacle avoidance using partial RGB-D observations, learned obstacle representations, trajectory prediction, and model predictive control.

  • Built a ROS 2 / Gazebo framework using RGB-D reconstruction and local point-cloud processing for online obstacle perception.
  • Estimated obstacle occupancy using PCA/MVEE initialization and residual neural-network refinement under occlusion and depth noise.
  • Combined Kalman-filter motion prediction with MPC-based spatial constraints for dynamic obstacle avoidance.
04 Sep 2024 — May 2025

Robotic Trajectory Planning for Aircraft CFRP Horizontal Stabilizer Rib Machining

Completed

Developed stiffness-aware robotic machining analysis and time-optimal trajectory planning for CFRP horizontal stabilizer rib manufacturing.

  • Derived ABB robot kinematics and a Jacobian-based static stiffness model for machining-pose analysis.
  • Developed a fifth-order polynomial trajectory planner optimized using particle swarm optimization, reducing predicted machining time by 40.8% relative to GA.
  • Validated trajectories in RobotStudio and optimized CFRP cutting parameters, reducing surface roughness by 13.6% to below 8.2 μm.
