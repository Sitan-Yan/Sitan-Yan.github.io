Reliability-Aware Shape-from-Focus for Reflective 3D Micro-Hole Measurement

Sitan Yan, W. Xu, L. Zeng, and W. Li

This project develops a microscopic shape-from-focus metrology system for reflective industrial micro-holes. The work combines focus-stack acquisition, reflection-aware preprocessing, reliability-aware reconstruction, and experimental validation against a commercial reference instrument.

  • Integrated a microscopic SFF measurement setup with 10× optics, polarizers, and translation stages.
  • Developed reflection-aware preprocessing and reliability-aware 3D reconstruction.
  • Validated the reconstructed geometry against an Alicona IFM G5 reference instrument.
