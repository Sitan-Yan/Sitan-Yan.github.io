Reliability-Aware Microscopic 3D Reconstruction System for Reflective Micro-Holes

This project investigates reliable microscopic shape-from-focus measurement of reflective micro-hole surfaces, where specular reflections can distort focus responses and degrade depth reconstruction.

I developed and experimentally evaluated a reliability-aware reconstruction framework that uses the estimated reliability of focus-derived observations to improve 3D surface recovery under reflective conditions.

Institution Huazhong University of Science and Technology
Laboratory State Key Laboratory of Digital Manufacturing Equipment and Technology
Advisor Prof. Wenlong Li
01

Overview

Microscopic shape-from-focus provides a non-contact approach for reconstructing micro-scale surfaces from images acquired at different focal positions. For reflective micro-hole surfaces, however, specular reflections can disturb the focus response and lead to unstable depth estimates, particularly near the entrance and inner wall.

The objective of this project is to improve the reliability of microscopic 3D reconstruction by explicitly considering the quality of focus-derived depth observations rather than treating all reconstructed points as equally reliable.

02

Experimental System

A microscopic shape-from-focus measurement platform was developed for acquiring focal stacks of reflective micro-hole samples.

The system integrates microscopic imaging, controlled axial scanning, illumination, and reference 3D measurement for experimental evaluation.

03

Approach

The reconstruction framework follows three high-level stages: focus-stack processing, reliability analysis of focus-derived depth observations, and reliability-guided 3D surface reconstruction.

1

Focus-Stack Processing

Focus information is extracted from microscopic image stacks acquired along the optical axis.

2

Reliability Analysis

The reliability of local focus-derived depth observations is evaluated under reflective imaging conditions.

3

3D Reconstruction

Reliability information is incorporated into surface recovery to suppress unstable local depth estimates.

04

Representative Results

The proposed framework was evaluated on reflective micro-hole samples using independent high-accuracy 3D measurements as reference. Representative results show improved surface continuity and reduced reconstruction artifacts near reflection-prone regions compared with conventional SFF reconstruction.
